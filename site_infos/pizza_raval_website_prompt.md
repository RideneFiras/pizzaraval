## Brand Identity & Tone

Pizza Raval is an Argentine-spirit spot living in the guts of El Raval. Think Buenos Aires meets Barcelona side street: handmade dough, empanadas folded by hand, milanesas the size of your face, and a bar that runs late and plays good music. It's cultural — events, art, skate vibes, local community.

They have 7,000+ Instagram followers and ZERO website. All orders go through WhatsApp. All reservations are DMs. The site needs to fix this.

**Tone:** Energetic, warm, Argentine confidence meets Raval roughness. Food first, culture second, vibe always. Not fine dining — a place you go on a Tuesday because you want something real.

**Language:** Spanish primary. Secondary English (lots of international visitors in El Raval).

## Design Specs

**Color palette:**
- Primary: deep tomato red `#C0392B` or `#E74C3C`
- Secondary: near-black `#1A1A1A`
- Cream/off-white: `#FDF6EC`
- Accent: golden yellow `#F1C40F` (for price callouts and badges)
- Text on dark: `#FFFFFF`

**Typography:**
- Headlines: `Bebas Neue` or `Anton` (Google Fonts) — condensed, bold, street-food energy
- Subheadings: `Oswald` medium or `Montserrat` bold
- Body / menu: `Inter` or `DM Sans`
- Prices: bold, oversized, accent color

**Layout feel:**
- Bold and unapologetic — like a Buenos Aires pizzeria menu
- Dark sections interspersed with cream sections for breathing room
- Menu tabs or accordion — not all stacked (it would be very long)
- Mobile-first — most orders happen on phones

**Textures:**
- Subtle paper/grain texture on cream sections (CSS noise filter or SVG pattern)
- Diagonal stripe accent in hero (Argentine flag nod)
- Red section dividers or zigzag borders

**Buttons:**
- Primary: red `#C0392B`, white text, rounded `6px`
- WhatsApp: `#25D366` green, white text, WhatsApp icon
- Secondary: outlined red
